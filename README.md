# FakeTrace

### AI-Powered Digital Content Verification & Threat Assessment Platform

FakeTrace is an AI-powered digital safety platform that helps users analyze suspicious digital content and understand potential risks before trusting, sharing, or acting on it.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://faketrace.vercel.app/  
📦 **GitHub:** https://github.com/shshnkdixit/FakeTrace

---

## 🎯 Problem

With the rapid growth of social media and AI-generated content, it is becoming harder for users to identify suspicious, misleading, or manipulated digital content.

Manual verification can be time-consuming and difficult for non-technical users.

## 💡 Solution

FakeTrace provides a simple interface where users can submit suspicious content and receive an AI-assisted risk assessment in an understandable format.
